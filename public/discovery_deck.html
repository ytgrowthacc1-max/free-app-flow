Step 1 of 4 • Community Stage FIRST

1. Understanding Your Community & Stage

ALWAYS start by asking about their community setup, niche, and launch status.

🚦

"What stage is your community currently at?"

Active community with existing paid members, or Pre-Launch / launching soon?

Key Insight Active = Retention focus. Pre-Launch = Hype & onboarding focus.
🎯

"What is your community niche & price point?"

Trading/Finance, Sports Betting, Reselling, SaaS/AI, Coaching, Gaming?

Key Insight Tailors app tools (signals, calculators, habit leaderboards).
👥

"How many members & what is your MRR goal?"

Current active member count and target growth over the next 90 days?

Key Insight Sets scale & database performance requirements.
🏆

"What is your #1 primary business goal right now?"

Reduce Churn, Boost Member Engagement, Automate Operations, or Launch a SaaS tool?

Key Insight Defines the main ROI objective for the app build.
Step 2 of 4 • Uncover Pain Points

2. Highlighting Where it Hurts Right Now

Identify what parts of their daily operations are eating up time or causing member loss.

⏳

"Where are you losing the most time or members?"

Are members churning after Month 1? Is manual onboarding too slow?

App Solution Gamified retention app & instant Whop OAuth onboarding.
🤖

"What manual task do you wish a tool did for you?"

Manual DMs, signal posting, spreadsheet tracking, managing access?

App Solution Automated dashboards, signal feeds, & webhook sync.
📉

"Where do members get confused or drop off?"

What habit or feature is missing in their first 7 days to build a daily routine?

App Solution Daily trackers, proprietary calculators, leaderboards.
💸

"What software tools are you currently paying for?"

Paying for multiple fragmented bots, CRMs, or external SaaS tools?

App Solution Consolidate into 1 custom app embedded inside Whop.
Step 3 of 4 • App Solution Scope

3. Designing the App to Solve the Pain

Mapping their specific community headache into a clean Day 1 custom tool.

🚀

Day 1 Core Tool

"What is the single most important dashboard or tool that fixes your biggest bottleneck on launch day?"

Focus Keep Version 1 lean & hyper-focused on solving main pain.
👤

User Roles & Access

"Who logs in (Admin, Staff, Paid Members), and what is the #1 action each person takes?"

Focus Simple 1-click views tailored to each role.
🔗

Integrations

"Which platforms (Whop, Stripe, Discord, Telegram, CRM) must connect automatically?"

Focus Automate data flow so you don't touch spreadsheets.
